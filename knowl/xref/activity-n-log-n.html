Activity 1.3.17.

Show that \(\displaystyle \sum _{n=2} ^\infty \frac{1}{n\ln(n)}\) diverges.
🔗
Solution.
Apply the Cauchy condensation test and look at
\begin{equation*} \displaystyle \sum _{n=1} ^\infty 2^n \frac{1}{2^n\ln(2^n)} = \sum _{n=1} ^\infty \frac{1}{n\ln(2)}=\frac{1}{\ln(2)} \sum _{n=1} ^\infty \frac{1}{n}\text{.} \end{equation*}
Since this is a multiple of the harmonic series, both this and the original series converge.
